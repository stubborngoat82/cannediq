Frequently asked questions

What is a "Stack"?

A Stack is a named group of Commands — like a folder for your most-used responses. For example, a "Support" stack with canned replies or a "Sales" stack with pitch snippets. Free users get 3 Stacks; Pro and above get unlimited.

How do AI Commands work?

AI Commands let you write a prompt template that's sent to GPT-4o mini (with Gemini as automatic fallback) and insert the result directly into any text field. Use {{inputText}} to reference the active field content. No API keys needed — AI is fully managed.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

Your commands and stacks stay in your local browser storage. You'll drop back to Free plan limits (25 commands, 3 stacks) but nothing is deleted. Export your full library first from the Options page.

Can I switch plans at any time?

Yes. Upgrades take effect instantly and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period. Use the Manage Billing button in the Options page to change plans, update payment, or cancel.

Is cannedIQ available on other browsers?

cannedIQ is currently a Chrome extension (Manifest V3). Firefox and Edge support is on the roadmap. All your data is portable via the export feature.

What does "AHPUSHIT LLC" mean on my statement?

AHPUSHIT LLC is the legal entity behind cannedIQ. You may see AHPUSHIT LLC or CANNEDIQ*AHPUSHIT on your credit card statement. This is expected and completely normal.

Do you offer refunds?

If you're unhappy within 14 days of your first charge, email us and we'll refund it — no questions asked. After that, refunds are handled case-by-case.
