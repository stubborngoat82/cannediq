How it works
Three steps. Zero friction.

No new app to learn. It lives inside whatever you're already typing in.

1

Click any text field

Works in Gmail, Intercom, Zendesk, LinkedIn, Slack web, Notion — any input, textarea, or contenteditable on any site.

2

Press Ctrl+Space

The launcher appears right below your cursor. Type to filter instantly across all your categories and responses.

3

Hit Enter to insert

Your response is pasted at the cursor, preserving everything already in the field. No copy-paste, no switching tabs.
